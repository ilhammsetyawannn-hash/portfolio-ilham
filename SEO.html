SEO • Case Study

SEO Website Optimization

A search engine optimization project focused on improving website visibility, strengthening on-page SEO, and developing a structured content strategy.

01 — OVERVIEW

Building Search Visibility

This project focused on improving the organic search visibility of a business website through structured SEO optimization and content development.

The approach combined on-page optimization, keyword targeting, content planning, and pillar-cluster strategy to create a stronger relationship between website pages and their target search queries.

02 — THE CHALLENGE

The Challenge

The website needed stronger organic visibility for strategic keywords. Several pages also required improvements in their SEO elements and content structure.

Search Visibility

Improve the visibility of targeted pages in organic search results.

Content Structure

Develop a more organized content structure around strategic topics and keywords.

On-Page SEO

Optimize important page-level SEO elements to create stronger search relevance.

03 — MY ROLE

My Role

I handled the SEO optimization and content-related activities required to improve the website's organic search performance.
