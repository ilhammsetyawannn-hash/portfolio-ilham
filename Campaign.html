01 — OVERVIEW

Turning Campaigns Into Business Opportunities

This project focused on developing and managing digital campaigns with the objective of generating relevant traffic, increasing audience interest, and ultimately creating qualified business leads.

The campaign approach combined audience targeting, creative development, campaign monitoring, and lead evaluation to ensure that marketing activities were connected with actual business opportunities.
